# Portfolio

Personal portfolio site showcasing work experience, projects, and skills.

## Language

### Experience

**Role**:
A single position held at a company — has a company, title, description, start date, and either an end date (past) or none (current).
_Avoid_: Job, position, experience entry

**Current role**:
The one Role presently held, with no end date. Exactly one at a time.
_Avoid_: Active role, ongoing role

**Past roles**:
All Roles other than the current one, ordered most-recent-first.
_Avoid_: Role history, previous jobs
